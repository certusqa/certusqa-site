The DeployShield Suite

Everything you need to ship with confidence. No partial solutions.

Automated Suite

Full regression coverage for up to 15 critical user flows — standard Playwright specs authored against your staging app, living in your repo. If you leave, the suite still runs.

CI/CD Integration

Native GitHub Actions or GitLab integration. Tests run on every PR, every merge.

Instant Alerts

Slack & Jira notifications with video replays, screenshots, and full error context.

Pre-Deploy Gate

A red gate means do not deploy covered flows. It ships in report-only — recording a verdict on every deploy and blocking nothing — until you decide to turn enforcement on.

The suite maintains itself — within limits we can state plainly
Your product changes every sprint. The tests keep up, without quietly going soft.

It fixes the test, never the verdict.

Drifted locators, moved flows, changed copy and timing races are repaired for you. A real regression or a broken fixture is reported and left alone — never rewritten.

It cannot force a test green.

No loosening an exact match, no downgrading a visibility check. That's a static check on the proposed diff, not a prompt instruction — deterministic rules plus an AST scan, no LLM calls. Two attempts, then it asks a person.

Your code never leaves your infra.

Runs on your own pipeline. Read-only until you say otherwise. Every capability is denied by default and switched on deliberately.
