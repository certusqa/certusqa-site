Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026 · Contact: hello@certusqa.com

Draft for design-partner use — not legal advice. This page describes how CertusQA handles information on the public site and in DeployShield delivery. Have counsel review before relying on it for regulated procurement.

Who we are

CertusQA (“we”) operates certusqa.com and delivers the DeployShield Suite and related QA services. The private console at app.certusqa.com is Access-gated (operators and invited client-readonly viewers only) and is not linked from this marketing site.

What we collect

  • Site visitors: standard server/CDN logs (IP, user agent, path) via Cloudflare. We do not load third-party analytics or ad trackers on this static site.
  • Intake & scheduling: if you use our Tally form or Calendly link, those providers process the fields you submit (name, email, company, notes).
  • Billing: Stripe processes payment details when you purchase DeployShield.
  • Client delivery: staging URLs, test credentials, CI logs, screenshots, and failure artifacts needed to run and maintain your suite — under your agreement and, where applicable, a DPA.

How we use information

To respond to inquiries, schedule kickoffs, bill for services, deliver and maintain automated regression coverage, and improve our service quality. We do not sell personal information.

AI tooling

When Premium / agent features are in scope, staging UI text and logs may be processed by AI tooling (including Cursor) as described in your agreement and our subprocessor list. We treat page content as untrusted and apply scrubbing controls where implemented. Agents target non-production environments unless a Production Rider is signed.

Retention

Marketing/lead records are kept as needed to respond to you. Evidence packs from delivery default to 90 days unless your agreement says otherwise, or you request earlier deletion.

Your choices

Email hello@certusqa.com to request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold about you as a prospect or client contact.

International & security

We use reputable subprocessors (see your agreement materials). The marketing site is static and served over HTTPS with HSTS. Client secrets must not be emailed in plaintext; use env/secret stores.

Changes

We may update this page; the “Last updated” date will change. Material changes for clients will be communicated as required by your agreement.
